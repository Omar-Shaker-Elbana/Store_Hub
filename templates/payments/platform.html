Why this account can't end the month negative

Couriers ($20 per trip) and warehouse workers ($10 per package) are paid from this account the moment they finish. The shopper pays a delivery & handling fee equal to exactly those costs ($20 × (stores + 1) + $10), collected with the order's cash on delivery. So every delivered order leaves this account with its 1% fee as pure profit; the only dips are short-lived advances on orders still in transit.
